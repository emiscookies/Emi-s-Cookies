About Emi's

baker's break 🐾

Hi, I'm Emi

Emi's Cookies started as a way to share what I love baking most: thick, chunky cookies that are a little underbaked in the middle on purpose. Every order is mixed and baked in small batches, so what you get is always fresh — never something that's been sitting around.

The Chunky Choc Chip and Cheesecake are the two I get asked for most, but everything on the menu is made the same careful way.

Health & hygiene, taken seriously

Every cookie, cake and drink from Emi's is prepared in a clean, dedicated baking space with food safety front of mind — not an afterthought.

  • Hands, tools and surfaces are washed and sanitised before every baking session
  • Ingredients are stored properly and checked for freshness before use, with allergens (nuts, dairy, gluten, egg) kept separate where possible
  • Each batch is baked to a safe internal temperature and cooled properly before packing
  • Cookies are packed in clean, sealed packaging as soon as they've cooled, so nothing is handled more than it needs to be
  • Got an allergy or dietary need? Let me know when you order and I'll flag anything you should be aware of

Questions, answered

How do I pay?

You can pay by PayNow or by cash on collection. Choose whichever you prefer at checkout.

When will I hear back after ordering?

We'll WhatsApp you to arrange a meetup time. If no one has contacted you within 2 working days, please message us on WhatsApp at 80382759.

How do I collect my order?

We arrange a meetup time and place with you over WhatsApp. Once it's sorted, you can double-check the details any time under “Check your order” below.

I have an allergy. Can I still order?

Let us know in the order notes when you check out and we'll flag anything you should be aware of. Allergens (nuts, dairy, gluten, egg) are kept separate where possible.

I didn't get my confirmation email.

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it's not there, message us on WhatsApp at 80382759 with your name and we'll sort it out.
